Phase 02: Optimisation

Improving functionality, accessibility, and overall performance based on feedback from the first prototype.

Description

Prototype 2 was developed as an enhanced version of the initial enclosure design. Following the evaluation of Prototype 1, several updates were implemented to improve the functionality, performance, and overall user experience of the case.

The revised design provides additional internal space for the ESP32-C3 board, improved lid alignment, and larger openings to allow easier access to the USB connection and GPIO pins.

Improvements from Prototype 1

  • More accurate fit for the ESP32 board
  • Enhanced lid fastening and alignment
  • Better access to the USB port and GPIO pins
  • Improved overall usability of the enclosure

Remaining Issues

  • Airflow and cooling could be further improved
  • The lid may become less secure with repeated use
  • Some edges require additional refinement for a smoother finish

Reflection

Prototype 2 demonstrated noticeable progress compared to the original design. The enclosure offered a more secure fit, improved accessibility, and a better overall user experience. Although the design addressed many of the previously identified issues, further modifications were still required before producing the final version.
