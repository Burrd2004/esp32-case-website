Phase 01: Initial Concept

The first stage in developing a durable and functional enclosure for the ESP32-C3 Zero.

Description

Prototype 1 is the first version of the ESP32-C3 case. It consists of two main parts: a base and a lid.

The base includes raised walls to hold the ESP32 board and cylindrical standoffs to support and secure the board in place.

The lid is a flat piece with four corner pins that align with the base to help keep the enclosure together.

Problems Identified

  • The lid may not securely attach
  • No ventilation for heat dissipation
  • Limited access to ports
  • Requires refinement for a better fit

Improvements Needed

  • Add ventilation holes
  • Improve lid locking mechanism
  • Adjust size for better fit
  • Increase access to ports and pins
