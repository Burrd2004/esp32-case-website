Final Release

The completed ESP32-C3 Zero enclosure, refined through testing, feedback, and multiple design iterations.

Overview

The Final Design represents the completed version of the ESP32-C3 case. Improvements from both Prototype 1 and Prototype 2 were incorporated to create a stronger, better-fitting, and more practical enclosure.

Key Features

Perfect Fit

Designed specifically for the ESP32-C3 board.

Improved Ventilation

Enhanced airflow helps reduce heat build-up.

Easy Access

USB ports and GPIO pins remain accessible.

Strong Structure

Improved durability and long-term reliability.
